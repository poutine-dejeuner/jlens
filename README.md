Anthropic’s Jacobian-lens paper [1] reports that concept directions in the residual stream of large language models are transported to the output in an approximately context-independent, linear way across a band of intermediate layers—an “emergent global workspace”—readable through a corpus-averaged Jacobian J¯ℓ = Ex [∂hfinal /∂hℓ ]. We study the evolution of statistics of the J-lens over the course of training of an LLM model.
